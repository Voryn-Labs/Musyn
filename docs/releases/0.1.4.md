**Musyn 0.1.4** update by **Voryn Labs**.

- **BitChord Top Charts Redesign**: Replaced legacy chart views with Apple Music-inspired featured hero banners, ranking badges, and proportional aspect-ratio protected playlist cards with smooth Cupertino responsive typography.
- **Liquid Download Progress Experience**: Overhauled the downloads queue with fluid tween-animated progress capsules, Apple Red gradient fill (`#FF375F` to `#FF2D55`), shimmer effects during indeterminate preparation, and symmetrical pill-styled retry/cancel controls.
- **Lock-Screen Playhead & Rapid Skip Fixes**: Resolved the lock-screen skip issue where skipping tracks carried over ghost playhead positions (e.g. 45s) from previous tracks by explicitly resetting AVPlayer source positions and indices upon track installation.
- **BitChord Frosted Drawer Architecture**: Completely eliminated ancestral dialogs and sheets across Source Selection, Downloads Manager, Settings Provider/Audio Quality, and Song Actions in favor of BitChord physics-driven frosted acrylic drawers.
- **Immersive Full-Stage Synced Lyrics**: Transformed the lyrics view into an expansive, Apple Music-style full-height stage over blurred artwork, with frosted header navigation and a streamlined compact transport dock.
- **Artwork Aspect-Ratio Protection**: Removed YouTube `hqdefault` letterboxing and bars, upgraded stream artworks to high-res square formats, and eliminated image distortion across the mini player, queue, and catalogue screens.
