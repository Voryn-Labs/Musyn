**Musyn 0.1.2** update by **Voryn Labs**.

- **JioSaavn & Catalog Downloads**: Fixed metadata dropping in `mediaItemToMap` and token validation in `makeSongOffline` so all JioSaavn and multi-source tracks download reliably with artwork and offline registration.
- **Lock-Screen Playback Continuity**: Removed remote HTTP clipping (`ClippingAudioSource`) and redundant seek calls, eliminating the 12–14s asset preparation stall on lock-screen and Control Center track skips.
- **Apple Music Scrubber**: Replaced the bulky default Cupertino slider thumb with a native, sleek Apple Music white scrubber thumb and smooth pan gesture tracker.
- **Top HUD Toast Pill**: Relocated status toast notifications to the top status bar area (Apple Dynamic Island / HUD capsule style), completely resolving collision with the Now Playing volume slider.
- **Frosted Modal Glass Sheets**: Added dark backdrop scrims and higher opacity to Source Picker and Download sheets to prevent background lyrics bleed-through, with unified native iOS sheet actions.
- **Home Screen Refresh Banner**: Replaced the invasive error block with a graceful, compact iOS retry banner that preserves feed visibility.
- **High-Contrast Synced Lyrics**: Tuned synced lyrics active line styling to crisp Apple Music high-contrast typography on blurred artwork canvas.

**Install:** iOS 15 or newer. `Musyn.ipa` is unsigned: sign it with a compatible installer or import it into a compatible LiveContainer version. Application `com.vorynlabs.musyn`, version 0.1.2 (3).
