# Musyn 0.3.7

**More music. Less waiting around.**

- **Sing Along is coming soon.** Its preview explains the planned vocal controls without starting a download or interrupting your music. Normal synced lyrics and lyric seeking remain available.
- **CarPlay browsing:** your queue, downloads, favorites and recent tracks, with Apple's Now Playing controls.
- **Phone-first playback:** CarPlay listeners and refresh timers stay inactive until a car connects. Stale selections and late connection events are handled safely.
- Refreshed player-menu wording, privacy information and feature previews.

CarPlay requires an approved audio entitlement in your signing profile. Musyn is an unsigned IPA for iOS 15+; sign it with a compatible installer. YouTube works without API keys, and JioSaavn remains optional with your own authorized key.
