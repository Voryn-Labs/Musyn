**Musyn 0.2.0** by **Voryn Labs**.

- Prepare lock-screen covers using native ImageIO/Core Graphics, without requiring a foreground Flutter rendering surface.
- Warm artwork for the next three queue entries and coalesce repeated cover requests. Keep stale artwork completions from replacing the selected track.
- Share pending stream resolution between warmup and a foreground tap. Warm one recent selection during launch without autoplay; downloaded tracks do not trigger a stream warmup.
- Skip the unnecessary JioSaavn title lookup when the selected track is already on the YouTube resolution path. Reduce the preferred forward buffer from five seconds to one, retaining the existing non-stalling-wait playback policy.
- Remove simulated moving sleeves and generated lock-screen artwork videos. The supplied reference animates the illustration inside its artwork; current YouTube/JioSaavn covers remain still. Gestures and player-sheet transitions remain available.
- Add separate cover, resolution and native-load timing diagnostics.

**Install:** iOS 15+. Unsigned `Musyn.ipa`, bundle `com.vorynlabs.musyn`, version **0.2.0 (11)**. Sign with your own identity using a compatible installer, or import into a compatible LiveContainer version.

Validation and measured device-log findings are recorded in `docs/DEVICE_LOG_AUDIT_0.2.0.md`. Network resolution and native buffering still vary by connection; these changes do not promise instant cold playback. Physical iPhone background artwork and startup latency require device acceptance.
